15 studies. See `../paradigms.md` for how to use this.

## duration judgment

- **Whether blinks shorten perceived duration to preserve visual continuity.** Compared: Perceived duration of blink blackouts vs artificial blackouts, and of stimuli interrupted by real vs simulated blinks vs uninterrupted.. Factors: real vs simulated blink, interruption duration. Marianne Duyck (2020), doi:10.1037/xhp0000864
- **How monetary incentives and metacognitive confidence relate to duration judgments.** Factors: monetary incentives. Mitra Taghizadeh Sarabi (2025), doi:10.5334/joc.414
- **how temporal context similarity shapes duration judgments.** Factors: temporal context. Jaume Boned (2022), doi:10.1038/s41598-022-27168-w

## action-effect learning with eye tracking

- **Anticipatory eye movements to action effects.** Compared: Anticipatory saccades to future effects for short vs long effect delays, spatially compatible vs incompatible locations, and relevant vs irrelevant effects.. Factors: effect delay, effect location compatibility, effect relevance. Florian Gouret (2023), doi:10.5334/joc.261
